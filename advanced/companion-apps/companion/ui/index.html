CrowPanel Companion

Calendar and music for your display. Settings close; the companion stays in your menu bar or system tray.

DEVICE CONNECTION

Disconnected

Use USB while developing, then switch to Bluetooth and wall USB power.

Pause releases USB so PlatformIO can upload. Close its serial monitor before resuming.

Switch from development to wireless
  1. In advanced/display, enable extensions.calendar or extensions.spotify in device.json, then build/upload using the existing PlatformIO tasks.
  2. Hold MENU for two seconds. With the board's back facing you and USB-C at the bottom, MENU is the small side button above the wheel.
  3. Choose Bluetooth, scan, select the CrowPanel name shown on the pairing screen, and connect. Enter its displayed code in the operating system's pairing prompt.
  4. Once connected, move the USB cable to a wall adapter. The laptop must stay awake and nearby for account updates.

On Windows, initial pairing in Bluetooth Settings may be needed. Paired devices reconnect without entering a new code. Hold EXIT for eight seconds to forget old bonds.

Spotify setup
  1. Create a Web API app in Spotify's developer dashboard.
  2. Register http://127.0.0.1:8766/callback and add your account to its user allowlist when required.
  3. Paste its public Client ID, enable Spotify, save, then sign in.

Development apps currently require a Premium app owner and allow five allowlisted users. A shared 60-person app needs approved access. Account setup is optional workshop prework.

VS Code owns your device.

This app connects to optional calendar and Spotify extensions in advanced/display. The default stock/weather screen runs on its own. Edit your screen content in VS Code. Build and upload with PlatformIO. This app supplies account data and the connection; it does not change your firmware or design.

While connected, enabled accounts update about every 30 seconds. Disconnected: no account reads; brief retries back off to five minutes. Use the menu-bar/tray menu for Start at sign-in or Quit. Tokens stay in OS credential storage.
